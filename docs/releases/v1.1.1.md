# MetaFolio 1.1.1

改进了学位论文识别。插件能从更多题名页读出具体学位和地点，也能从版权页提取 ISBN。遇到德国高校的审批页，院系批准日会显示在核对提示中，方便你决定条目该填哪个日期。

Thesis recognition now picks up degree names and places from more title pages, as well as ISBNs from copyright pages. On German approval sheets, the faculty approval date appears in a review note so you can choose the appropriate date for your citation.
